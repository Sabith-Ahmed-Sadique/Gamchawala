How our business works

E-commerce category: B2C. Gamchawala is our own brand. We buy finished gamchas from handloom weavers at a fair price and sell them directly to individual customers through this website.

Revenue model: direct sales. We earn the margin on every gamcha sold (Tk 320 to Tk 1,450), plus festival gift bundles for Pohela Boishakh and Eid.

Why it works: Every Bangladeshi owns a gamcha, but the ones in the bazaar all look the same. We turn it into a heritage product people are proud to wear, gift and post online, while giving rural weavers a steady market.

Key Partners

Handloom weaver cooperatives in Sirajganj and Kushtia, nakshi kantha artisans, bKash and Nagad, SSLCommerz, Pathao Courier.

Key Activities

Designing new patterns each season, quality checking every piece, product photography, packaging and social media.

Key Resources

Our weaver network, in-house design team, this website and the Gamchawala brand story.

Value Proposition

Pure cotton, handwoven gamchas with heritage designs you will not find in any bazaar, each tagged with the name of the weaver who made it.

Customer Relationships

A weaver story card in every parcel, a Facebook community, and early access to festival editions for members.

Channels

This website, Facebook and Instagram, and pop-up stalls at Boishakhi melas and university fests.

Customer Segments

Urban Bangladeshis aged 18 to 35 who love heritage fashion, and anyone buying a meaningful gift.

Cost Structure

Fair payments to weavers, cotton yarn and natural dyes, packaging, courier charges, Facebook ads and hosting.

Revenue Streams

Direct sales of gamchas (Tk 320 to Tk 1,450) and festival gift bundles.
