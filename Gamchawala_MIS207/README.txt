Business
--------
Name: Gamchawala
E-commerce category: B2C
Revenue model: Direct sales

Gamchawala is a Bangladeshi brand that sells handwoven cotton gamchas with
heritage designs (alpona, nakshi kantha, jamdani, rickshaw art and nature
themes) directly to individual customers through its website (B2C). We buy
each piece from handloom weavers at a fair price and earn the margin on every
gamcha and festival gift bundle we sell.

How to open
-----------
Unzip the folder and open index.html in any modern browser. No server or
internet connection is needed. On the home page, type "gamcha" in the popup
box to enter the store.
